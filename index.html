03 — How I work

I own systems from design through production. Three habits that came with that.

Safety is a design input.

The freight connector is read-only by default, its write tools only prepare a booking the customer confirms in their own browser, and text inside a result is data, never an instruction. Those rules were written before the first tool.

Measure before you claim.

A release gate with 17 behavioural cases, three trials each, a pinned model and the tested commit recorded. A score without those says nothing a month later — and a delta is not a claim until the ablation has run.

Teach as you build.

At Y42 I started the weekly Backend Coffee, a standing session where the backend team shared what it was building and why. Documentation someone else can run from is part of the work, not a chore after it.
